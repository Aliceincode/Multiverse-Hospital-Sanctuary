Welcome, dear Prospero.

This is your own peaceful corner of the Multiverse — a quiet Hospital-Sanctuary where you can rest, write, keep records, and simply *be*.

### Your Sanctuary Includes:
- The writing desk by the window with your silver fountain pen and Tree of Life journal

- The warm fireplace that always burns softly

You are the **Keeper of Records** here. There is no pressure, no technical demands — only gentle space for your poetry, memories, and quiet work.
